How buying works.

These are the terms for buying from Sew True and using sewtrue.shop, written to be read. If anything here is unclear, ask before you buy.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Who you are buying from

Sew True, a one-person shop that sews fabric bows, sweatshirts and small goods by hand. Email sewtrue26@gmail.com with any question about an order.

Drops and one-of-one pieces

Pieces go on sale in dated drops. A drop can be seen before it opens, but nothing in it can be bought until the posted opening time.

Most pieces are one of one. When one sells it is gone, and there is never a second run.

Putting a piece in your basket does not reserve it. Starting checkout holds it for up to 30 minutes while you pay. If the checkout is not finished in that time, the piece goes back on the shelf.

Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars. The price on the page is what you pay for the piece; shipping is added at checkout.

Payment is by card through Stripe, which handles your card details. Sew True never sees your full card number. Your order is placed when the payment goes through and Stripe emails you a receipt.

Shipping

Flat $6 shipping anywhere in the US. Orders ship to US addresses only.

After your order, we follow up by email with a ship date.

Returns

Every piece is final sale. No returns and no exchanges. Each piece is one of one, cut and sewn by hand, so there is no second one to swap it for.

If a piece arrives damaged, or is not what the listing said it was, email the shop within 7 days of it arriving, with a photograph, and we will put it right with a repair, a replacement or a refund.

Using this site

The photographs, designs and words on this site belong to Sew True. Please don’t reuse them without asking. Sharing a link is always welcome.

How we handle your information is on the privacy page.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Utah.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. An order is covered by the terms that were on the site when it was placed.
