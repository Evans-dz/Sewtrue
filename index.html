    About

    One machine, one pair of hands.

    Sew True is a one-person shop. Fabric gets picked a bolt at a time, cut on the grain, pressed, sewn and photographed on the same front door you see in every picture.

    That's the whole operation, and it's why the numbers are small and the drops are dated.

    Will it live outside

    Yes, under a covered porch. Cotton fades in direct desert sun, so bring it in if the door faces west and it's July.

    Can I hang it on something other than a door

    The strap fits a wreath hanger, a stair rail, a mailbox post, a truck grille and the back of a chair at a party. The mini also clips into hair.
